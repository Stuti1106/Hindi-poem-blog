Far away, in some corner of a garden,
a little plant swayed in the joy of youth,

its arms spread toward the sky, reaching new heights day after day.

Tender shoots of new waves and new hopes adorned it, humming softly,

laughing with love through winter, autumn, the cold season, sunshine, rain and spring.

The wheel of time and circumstance turned at its own pace.

Today that plant seemed happier than happiness itself.

Something had happened that brought a glow to its colour:
today, in its lap, a tiny bud
had smiled.

Holding her close in its arms, it leapt with joy,
and swaying with the breeze, lost in love, it overflowed with tenderness.

It was proud, thinking it was the luckiest in the whole garden.

The tender bud, too, finding herself safe in those arms, slowly woke from her sleep,
opening her tiny petals and smiling softly.

In time the bud took on her own form,
and though glad to see her bloom, the plant's heart became a home for many worries.

Worry about the tomorrow to come,
about the moment she would part and go far away.

The one it had cared for so carefully,
raised with so much love and indulgence:

she who is the brightness of this place today
will become the light of the whole world tomorrow.

Whoever takes her far from my sight tomorrow,
will he cherish her as tenderly as I have?

How many duties she will have to fulfil,
how many wounds she will have to bear.

This custom of the world must be kept;
the bud must go far away to do her life's work.

Sometimes she will stumble, sometimes she will wilt,
but the lessons and understanding I have given her will become her shield.

She who is the fragrance of this place today will scent the whole world tomorrow;
she who smiles in these arms today will fill the whole world tomorrow.

Thinking this, the plant smiled, caressed the bud, and said,

"O little bud, never think of yourself as weak;
every moment, whatever comes, know that I am with you.

It is with courage and wisdom that a name is made;
however deep the darkness may be,
the flame of one small lamp spreads light.

So never make yourself small, nor think yourself less;
hold the reins of joy on the chariot of life, and keep stepping toward success, keep moving on."
