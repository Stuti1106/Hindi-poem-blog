Win over the mind, and you have won;
lose to the mind, and you have lost.

Whoever has learned this secret
crosses safely to the far shore, brother, safely to the far shore.

Whoever has held the mind in check
has become the crown of the world.

Whoever is tangled in the mind's knots
has wasted a whole life,
brother, wasted a whole life.

This is life's guiding mantra;
whoever understands it is wise.

Whoever gets caught in tricks and schemes
is a foolish, unknowing soul.

The mind is restless, the mind is tender, the mind is innocent;
the mind is a tent pitched among doubts.

The mind is strength, the mind is devotion;
the mind is a circle of feelings.

The mind is a riddle no one can read;
whoever solves it is saved.
Whoever cannot is stranded midstream,
brother, stranded midstream.
